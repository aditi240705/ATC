Mini-Itineraries for Your Next Escape

Choose a destination to see a sample itinerary. Every plan can be tailored to your dates, budget, and interests.

Bali, Indonesia

Island of the Gods

Duration5 Nights / 6 Days
Day 1Arrival & Sunset Dinner

Arrive at Denpasar (DPS) and transfer privately to Seminyak. Evening sunset dinner by the beach.

Day 2Rice Terraces & Sacred Monkey Forest

Drive to Ubud, visit the Tegallalang rice terraces and the Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary.

Day 3Cultural Temple Tour

Visit the holy spring temple at Tirta Empul, join a Balinese cooking class, and end with a traditional dance performance.

Day 4Clifftop Temple & Kecak Dance

Explore Uluwatu Temple on the cliffs, then watch the Kecak fire dance at sunset.

Day 5Nusa Penida Island Trip

Speedboat to Nusa Penida for the Kelingking Beach viewpoints and snorkelling.

Day 6Leisure & Departure

Free morning for shopping or a spa, then transfer to the airport.

Japan

Tokyo, Hakone & Kyoto

Duration6 Nights / 7 Days
Day 1Arrive in Tokyo

Airport transfer to Shinjuku. Evening walk through the bars of Golden Gai.

Day 2Tokyo Highlights

Senso-ji Temple in Asakusa, Shibuya Crossing, and the peaceful Meiji Shrine.

Day 3Hakone Mountain Escape

Scenic train to Hakone, Mount Fuji views from Lake Ashi, and a night in a hot spring ryokan.

Day 4Shinkansen to Kyoto

Bullet train to Kyoto, then the thousand torii gates of Fushimi Inari at dusk.

Day 5Kyoto Temples & Gion

Arashiyama bamboo grove, the Golden Pavilion (Kinkaku-ji), and an evening stroll through Gion.

Day 6Nara Day Trip

Meet the deer at Nara Park and visit Todai-ji's great Buddha hall. Return to Osaka for the evening.

Day 7Dotonbori & Departure

Food tour of Dotonbori in Osaka, then transfer to Kansai Airport.

Jeju Island, South Korea

Volcanic Island Getaway

Duration4 Nights / 5 Days
Day 1Arrival & Black Pork Dinner

Airport pickup and a welcome dinner of Jeju black pork barbecue.

Day 2Sunrise at Seongsan Ilchulbong

Climb the volcanic crater at sunrise, then walk part of the Olle coastal trail.

Day 3Lava Tubes & Waterfalls

Explore the Manjanggul lava tube and the Jeongbang Waterfall, where the sea meets the falls.

Day 4Beaches & Coastal Cafés

Hyeopjae Beach, the Oedolgae rock formation, and a relaxed tour of seaside cafés.

Day 5Departure

Transfer to Jeju airport for your flight.

Switzerland

Lakes & Alpine Peaks

Duration6 Nights / 7 Days
Day 1Arrive in Zurich

Airport transfer and a scenic boat cruise on Lake Zurich.

Day 2Lucerne & Mount Pilatus

Walk across the Chapel Bridge in Lucerne, then take the cable car up Mount Pilatus.

Day 3Interlaken Adventure

Scenic train through the Bernese Oberland, with an evening by the lake in Interlaken.

Day 4Top of Europe

Rail up to Jungfraujoch, with snowfields and the Sphinx observation deck.

Day 5Zermatt & the Matterhorn

Car-free alpine village of Zermatt and the Glacier Paradise viewpoint.

Day 6Glacier Express

Panoramic train journey to St. Moritz through alpine valleys and viaducts.

Day 7Departure

Transfer to Zurich airport for your flight.

Maldives

Overwater Serenity

Duration4 Nights / 5 Days
Day 1Seaplane Arrival & Sunset

Seaplane transfer to your overwater villa, with sunset welcome drinks.

Day 2House Reef Snorkelling

Guided snorkel over the house reef, with tropical fish and coral gardens.

Day 3Dolphin Cruise & Sandbank Picnic

Sunrise dolphin watching and a private picnic on a sandbank.

Day 4Spa & Overwater Dinner

Spa treatment followed by dinner on the water under the stars.

Day 5Departure

Seaplane transfer to Malé for your flight.

Dubai, UAE

Desert Dunes & City Lights

Duration4 Nights / 5 Days
Day 1Arrival & Marina Dinner Cruise

Hotel check-in, then an evening dhow dinner cruise along Dubai Marina.

Day 2Dubai Icons

Burj Khalifa observation deck, Dubai Mall, and the Dubai Fountain show.

Day 3Desert Safari

Dune bashing, a camel ride, and a barbecue dinner under the stars.

Day 4Abu Dhabi Day Trip

Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque and Louvre Abu Dhabi.

Day 5Departure

Airport transfer for your flight.

Singapore

The Garden City Escape

Duration4 Nights / 5 Days
Day 1Arrival & Marina Bay Skyline

Hotel check-in, then skyline views from the Marina Bay area at night.

Day 2Gardens & Supertrees

Gardens by the Bay, the Flower Dome and Cloud Forest, and the evening light show.

Day 3Sentosa Island

Full day on Sentosa with beaches and Universal Studios Singapore.

Day 4Hawker Food & Heritage

Chinatown, Little India, and a local hawker food tour.

Day 5Departure

Transfer to Changi Airport for your flight.
